An exploratory, island-wide study of announced markets and commercial space. A research sample, not a complete census or live market guide.

What is being counted?

The baseline is the frozen v8 research snapshot, as of 23 October 2026. It contains 120 core market episodes at 92 distinct site IDs; 119 episodes have representative coordinates. One site may host several episodes. Programmes, exhibitions, historical comparisons and unadopted procurement leads are not added to the core count.

“Past” and “within window” describe announced dates relative to the snapshot, not verified operation. Future announcements remain separate. Filters preserve incomplete dates as uncertainty. Month filters include known intersecting date ranges; uncertain records that cannot be assigned to a month are excluded from that month and reported separately.

Commercial buildings & spatial measures

2,525 archived HDB commercial=Y address records are linked to points; 2,524 have official HDB building footprints. These are building addresses, not shop units, ODA permits or a national inventory of all commercial property. The remaining address is point-only and never rendered as a footprint.

Distances and buffers are computed in SVY21 (EPSG:3414), in metres. Light geometry shows every HDB commercial building. Dark geometry highlights buildings whose footprints are at most 50 m from a filtered market representative point; the point-only record uses point distance. In chapter 02, selecting a market restricts the dark layer to that market. This fixed threshold is a display rule, not a market boundary. No search-radius control is exposed. Earlier sensitivity analyses are retained in the research project, outside the website. Market coordinates are landmark/building proxies, not entrances or surveyed boundaries. A zero distance does not establish physical co-location.

Planning-area summaries use URA MP2019 boundaries. Commercial address points and market representative points determine area assignment. “No collected market” means no record in this sample, not proven absence.

How potential ODA is modelled

The displayed ODA candidate layer is fixed at 2 m: an external band around each commercial footprint, with all intersecting known HDB footprints subtracted. The archived 1 and 3 m sensitivity outputs remain available for reproducibility. Widths are researcher-selected sensitivity settings, not statutory display depths, observed yellow boxes or confidence intervals. Rounded external corners follow the buffer geometry.

Archived area metrics are dissolved before summation and clipped to their stated analysis circles. Area metrics are not shown in the market information panel. Different building candidate bands can overlap; the union avoids double counting. Only known HDB footprints are obstacles: roads, non-HDB buildings, property control and other obstructions remain untested. Full-perimeter bands do not identify shopfront direction and can omit covered corridors inside the mapped footprint. Metre-scale shapes are illustrative; source accuracy has not been surveyed.

The model identifies candidate vicinity only. There are no verified ODA permit geometries and no actual market footprints. No overlap percentage, compliance finding or actual occupied area is calculated.

What do brand connections mean?

Connections join distinct sites at which the same recorded brand-name key appears in public sources. Different spellings and unresolved identities are kept separate. Lines have no arrows or chronological travel meaning. An event’s time window does not prove daily vendor attendance. Several outlets or teams may operate at once.

Month, area and status filters also apply to brand appearances. The list defaults to brands with at least two matching sites; turn off the multi-site filter to see single-site names.
